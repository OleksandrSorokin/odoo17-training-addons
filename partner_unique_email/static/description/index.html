Partner Unique Email

Clean contact records, reliable communication

Prevent duplicate email addresses across all Odoo Contacts.

  • Case-insensitive and whitespace-tolerant uniqueness checks.
  • Helpful validation messages on saving partners.
  • SQL unique index also protects against simultaneous updates.
  • Blank email fields remain allowed.

Compatibility: Odoo 17 Community and Enterprise (base module).

Before installation: remove existing duplicate email addresses. The module will not silently delete or merge contacts.
